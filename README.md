# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 01 — Conversion Opportunity

Out of **10,000 customers, 4,277 purchased travel plans**, resulting in an observed purchase rate of **42.8%**.

This highlights an opportunity to improve conversion among customers who have not yet purchased.

### 02 — High-Value Age Segment

The **26–45 age group** emerged as an important target segment for travel-plan campaigns.

### 03 — Income-Based Opportunity

Middle-income and upper-middle-income customers represented valuable segments for targeted marketing initiatives.

### 04 — Frequent Traveler Opportunity

Frequent travelers represent an important opportunity for:

- Repeat purchases
- Loyalty programs
- Retention strategies
- Personalized offers

### 05 — Digital Booking Opportunity

Improving digital booking adoption can simplify the customer journey and support scalable customer acquisition.
